About Phil

Experience, curiosity and a dislike of boring corporate content.

I have spent decades creating photography and film for organisations that need to explain what they do, why it matters and why people should care.

My background is in commercial photography, filmmaking and visual storytelling. I work with companies ranging from technical specialists to global organisations, helping them turn complex subjects into clear, engaging content.

The process is collaborative and practical. I ask questions, understand the audience and find the human story behind the technology, products or people.

The result is content that feels authentic rather than manufactured, because the best corporate stories are usually the real ones.
